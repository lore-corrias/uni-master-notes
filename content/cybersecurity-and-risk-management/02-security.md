# Restrictions and Limitations

In order to avoid risks and increase security, a technician always has to balance security mechanisms with inconvenience for the company or the employees.

Security is actually a _process_: it is not enough to put defense measures in place and leave it at that, as these need to _continuously_ be integrated and improved. 

Defense measures are typically called _security policies_. The security policies are part of a system that interacts with itself, the context and other assets. Of course, these interactions might fail when there is a threat, but even if there is one. We call these cases, respectively, _passive failure_ and _active failure_.

# CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a triad of principles that are the fundamental goals when handling the security of a system. They are:

* Confidentiality
* Integrity
* Availability

The order of importance is: availability, integrity and confidentiality.

Another updated version of this triad incorporates also _authenticity_ and _accountability_.
